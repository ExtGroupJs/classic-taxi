{% trans "What to do in Cayo Coco and Cayo Guillermo" %}

{% trans "Cayo Coco and Cayo Guillermo are two Caribbean destinations in Cuba that offer a perfect combination of nature, adventure, and relaxation. Here are the most recommended excursions and places to enjoy your visit to the fullest:" %}

{% trans "Bagá Natural Park (Cayo Coco)" %}

{% trans "A must-see for nature lovers. With almost 13 hectares, you can observe flamingos, turtles, and other native species in their natural habitat. Ideal for ecotourism and wildlife photography." %}

{% trans "Pilar Beach (Cayo Guillermo)" %}

{% trans "Considered one of the most beautiful beaches in the world, it stands out for its white sand, dunes, and crystal-clear waters. Perfect for relaxing and enjoying the almost pristine natural surroundings." %}

{% trans "Diving and Snorkeling on the Cayo Coco Reefs" %}

{% trans "Discover vibrant marine life in warm, shallow waters. Areas like La Jaula, Las Coloradas, and Casasa are ideal for diving and snorkeling." %}

{% trans "Boat Adventures and Catamaran Tours" %}

{% trans "Cruise the canals south of Cayo Coco and near Cayo Guillermo. You can drive boats or enjoy catamaran rides, and snorkel in less crowded areas." %}

{% trans "Cayo Coco Dolphinarium" %}

{% trans "Experience swimming and playing with dolphins. Ideal for families and those who want close contact with these marine mammals." %}

{% trans "Rocarena Climbing Center" %}

{% trans "A modern climbing wall in Cayo Coco with levels for everyone. Perfect for adding a touch of adrenaline and sport to your stay." %}

{% trans "Laguna de Leche and El Guanaroca Natural Park" %}

{% trans "Laguna de Leche, the largest lagoon in Cuba, and Guanaroca Park are ideal for birdwatching and exploring unique ecosystems." %}

{% trans "Less crowded beaches and complementary activities" %}

{% trans "Explore beaches like Las Coloradas, Playa Larga, Playa Flamencos, and El Paso. You can organize horseback riding tours, shark-watching excursions, and water sports like kayaking, windsurfing, and water skiing." %}
